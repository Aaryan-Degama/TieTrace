06 / Editorial gatekeeping

Who decides: a journalist approves every note.

The model never records a verdict and never approves a sourcing note. TieTrace provides the evidence trail and puts a human checkpoint between the graph data and your published article.

  • A sourcing note can be approved only after the Verifier checks every claim.
  • Verdicts and approvals need a second, confirming click.
  • The reviewer’s name is recorded with each decision.

07 / Forensic boundaries

Strict ethical boundaries.

We say “ties found in the research record”. We present documented relationships and never allege motive.

What this establishes

  1. Disclosed funding: a funder named on a paper, with its DOI.
  2. Declared affiliations: an affiliation listed on a paper.
  3. Traceability: every tie maps to a stored edge and a DOI.

What it does not prove

  1. Bias or wrongdoing: sponsorship is not proof of altered results.
  2. Editorial influence: ties do not show a funder shaped the conclusions.
  3. Right of reply: findings are leads that need a formal inquiry before publication.
